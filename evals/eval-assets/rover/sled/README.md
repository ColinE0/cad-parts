# IR sled

Holds the IR line board under the front of the base plate. The flange bolts up against the
plate's underside through its two sled holes (M3 x 12 down from the top, nuts in the hex pockets
under the flange); the board screws up into the underside of the lip.

| Version | What changed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| v14 | gussets between the flange and the lip | printed 2026-09-27, lip flexed less, fitted |
| v15 | nut pockets instead of self-tappers | printed 2026-10-06, fitted |
| v16 | lip 4 mm deeper: emitters about 7 mm off the floor instead of 11 | trial; fit checks passed 2026-10-20 |

v14 and v15 are not kept here.
